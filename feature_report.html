2. Ingestion & Inbox Monitoring

  • Automated Gmail Inbox Polling: Connects securely using official Google API (gmail/v1) with OAuth 2.0, querying for unread buyer inquiries (is:unread label:INBOX).
  • Intelligent Daemon & Spam Filtering: Utilizes an active exclusion list (IGNORE_KEYWORDS) to automatically skip and silence non-actionable emails (no-reply, alerts@, mailer-daemon, donotreply, google.com, etc.).
  • Zero-Duplicate Archiving: Once an inquiry is handled, the pipeline automatically strips the UNREAD label to prevent reprocessing loops and keep your inbox organized.

3. AI Intelligence & Lead Qualification

  • Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash: Ultra-fast reasoning and contextual understanding on unstructured email messages, subject lines, and sender signatures.
  • Strict Pydantic JSON Schema: Guarantees zero hallucinations through typed schema validation:
    • sender_name: Full prospect name extracted from greeting, body, or metadata.
    • company_name: Detected business or firm (defaults to 'Individual').
    • inquiry_category: Categorized into Enterprise Sales, Product Support, Partnership, or General Inquiry.
    • urgency_level: Evaluated priority grade (High, Medium, or Low).
    • summary_intent: Concise 1-sentence breakdown of what the prospect needs.
    • recommended_reply: Context-aware personalized reply draft addressing specific inquiries.
  • High-Demand Resilience (503 Auto-Retry): Handles transient API throttling with automated exponential backoff up to 5 successive attempts.

5. Instant Context-Aware Auto-Reply Dispatcher

  • Brand Voice & Persona: Configured for Sam Jewels with strict professional standards: double line-breaks after greeting, 1–2 focused readable paragraphs, and clean corporate sign-offs.
  • Clean HTML Styling: Automatically converts raw text newlines into web-safe HTML formatting (Arial, 14px font, 1.6 line height) to render consistently on mobile and desktop email clients.
  • Thread Continuity (threadId): Replies directly inside the customer's existing thread, preserving full context and subject history (Re: [Subject]).
